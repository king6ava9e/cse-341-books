## Plan vs. Implementation

5. Which part of the work took more or less time than expected? Why?

Answer:The MongoDB setup and connecting the books and authors through authorId took more time than I expected. I had some connection issues with MongoDB Atlas, and I also needed time to understand how the two collections relate. The CRUD operations and Swagger documentation were more straightforward once the structure was in place.

6. How did Swagger help you in your work this week?

Answer:Swagger made it easier to test my API endpoints without needing a separate tool. I could send GET, POST, PUT and DELETE requests, check the responses and status codes, and quickly identify problems. It also helped me document my API so that the available endpoints and how to use them were clear.
